# Suno prompts — Brawl Street fight music (fast set only)

**The vibe, read off the eleven references.** The Tekken 4 tracks are jazzy drum and bass and
funk breakbeat around 170 — fast, bouncy, cool, never heavy. The rap ones are all the smooth
kind: smoky jazz chords, glossy bells, dreamy pads. Put together: **fast breaks with jazzy
chords and relaxed swagger**. Head-nod music that moves quickly, not battle music.

**The trick that matters:** asking for "trap" at a fast BPM gives you half-time, which feels
slow and heavy — that is why the earlier takes came out wrong. Ask for **jungle, drum and
bass or breakbeat** and you get real speed with a light touch.

**Keep in:** smooth, bouncy, jazzy, glossy, dreamy, breezy, cool, nostalgic, groovy
**Keep out:** aggressive, menacing, dark, epic, battle, boss, heavy, brutal, trap, half time

**Running them in Suno**
- **Instrumental** on. Prompt in the **Styles** box, lyrics box empty.
- Exclude styles: `vocals, singing, speech, trap, half time, aggressive, metal, cinematic, horror`
- 2–3 takes each, keep the best. Aim for 2 minutes; the game loops them.
- Rights: only tracks made on a **paid** Suno plan are cleared for commercial use.

## THE FORMULA (settled 9/20, after two wrong turns)

**Fast genre + momentum phrase FIRST · jazzy melody SECOND · warm mix THIRD.**

1. Genre must be a fast one: `jungle`, `drum and bass`, `jungle breakbeat`. Never `soulful
   breakbeat`, `hip hop`, `boom bap`, `lo-fi`, `trip hop` — those pull it to 90 BPM whatever
   number you give it, which is why early takes came back "slow as fuck old school hip hop".
2. Say the momentum out loud: `continuous double-time energy`, `continuous full-time
   breakbeat momentum`, `no half time`. Without it Suno puts the snare on every other bar and
   172 sounds like 86.
3. The melody should MOVE SLOWLY while the drums move fast (`slow moving cello line` over 177
   BPM drums). That contrast is the whole calm-but-quick effect Duane is after.
4. Mix words that earned the winners: `spacious wide mix with warm golden saturation`,
   `dusty tape texture and restrained mixing`, `gently sidechained`, `washed reverb`.
5. Exclude styles: `vocals, singing, speech, hip hop, boom bap, lo-fi, trip hop, downtempo,
   chillhop, half time, aggressive, cinematic`

**After you download them**

1. Convert to the container the game serves (`.m4a` is not served by the artifact host):
   `ffmpeg -i track.mp3 -c:a aac -b:a 160k -movflags +faststart track.mp4`
2. Add each one to `playlist.json` in this folder:
   `{ "file": "airport_break.mp4", "title": "Airport Break", "credit": "" }`
   Leave `credit` empty for AI-generated tracks — nobody to credit.
